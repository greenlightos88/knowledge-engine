# Research Provenance and Intake

## Purpose

Research should increase specificity and credibility without becoming copied surface texture, unsupported authority or lore accumulation.

## Source record

For each material source record:

```yaml
source:
  title: ""
  creator_or_institution: ""
  publication: ""
  date: ""
  source_type: "primary | scholarly | journalistic | interview | reference | anecdotal"
  location_or_link: ""
  accessed: ""
  authority_notes: ""
  limitations: ""

use:
  claims_supported: []
  project_relevance: ""
  dramatic_application: ""
  cultural_or_ethical_risks: []
  quotation_status: "paraphrase | short quotation cleared | do not quote"
```

## Research rules

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources for factual claims.
- Separate verified fact, contested interpretation, personal testimony and creative invention.
- Do not treat a single person's experience as representative of a culture.
- Preserve uncertainty when sources disagree.
- Translate research into behaviour, institutions, material conditions, language context and consequence—not decorative facts.
- Never copy a source's distinctive expression, scene construction or protected text into generated work.
- For living cultures and specialized practices, identify where consultation remains necessary.

## Dramatic integration test

Before adding research-derived detail ask:

1. Does it affect a character's choices or worldview?
2. Does it alter the world's constraints or power structure?
3. Does it create a more precise image, behaviour or conflict?
4. Is it comprehensible in context without a lecture?
5. Is its source and uncertainty recorded?

If not, keep it in research notes rather than forcing it into the story.
