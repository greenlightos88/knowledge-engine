# Human Writing Gate

Run this gate after scene architecture is sound and before prose is approved.

A polished draft can still fail. This gate tests whether the work feels selected by a particular human consciousness rather than assembled from common narrative patterns.

## Automatic rejection conditions

Reject the passage when any of the following materially affects the page:

- characters explain emotions already evident in action;
- multiple characters share the same syntax, wit, insight, or metaphor system;
- description inventories a room without viewpoint selection;
- emotional intensity depends mainly on fragments, ellipses, em dashes, trembling, clenched jaws, held breath, or pounding hearts;
- dialogue exists primarily to brief the audience;
- every exchange answers the exact question asked;
- the prose uses vague cinematic adjectives instead of observable evidence;
- a twist depends on unfairly hidden causality;
- poetic language cannot be traced to viewpoint, world, or thematic pressure;
- every beat lands with equal emphasis;
- the scene states its meaning after behaviour or imagery has already conveyed it;
- a detail, line, or beat could transfer unchanged into many unrelated stories.

## Scored review

Score each category from 0 to 2.

### 1. Particular consciousness

0 — No identifiable selector of detail.

1 — Viewpoint is present but inconsistent or generic.

2 — Attention, bias, memory, and language clearly belong to this consciousness.

### 2. Behavioural truth

0 — Emotion is labelled or performed through stock shorthand.

1 — Some behaviour carries emotion, but explanation remains.

2 — Strategy, contradiction, timing, and consequence make emotion legible.

### 3. Voice separation

0 — Lines are interchangeable.

1 — Surface differences exist.

2 — Characters differ in thought pattern, social tactic, articulation, and silence.

### 4. Specificity

0 — Details are vague, decorative, or transferable.

1 — Some grounded details matter.

2 — Selected details reveal action, relationship, culture, or pressure.

### 5. Rhythm

0 — Uniform cadence or manufactured intensity.

1 — Some variation, with visible habits.

2 — Sentence and scene rhythm respond naturally to consciousness and pressure.

### 6. Negative space

0 — Meaning is fully explained.

1 — Some inference is permitted.

2 — Behaviour, omission, and silence carry essential meaning without obscuring causality.

### 7. Originality and inevitability

0 — Generic, derivative, or arbitrary.

1 — Familiar construction with project-specific elements.

2 — Choices feel surprising, causally earned, and inseparable from this project.

## Passing threshold

- No automatic rejection condition may remain unresolved.
- No category may score 0.
- Total score must be at least 11 out of 14.
- Particular consciousness, behavioural truth, and voice separation must each score 2 for character-centred scenes.

## Repair order

Do not begin with synonyms.

1. repair viewpoint and dramatic pressure;
2. repair character strategy and contradiction;
3. repair selection of detail;
4. remove explanation;
5. differentiate voice and articulation;
6. reshape rhythm;
7. perform a final compression pass.

Record major recurring failures in the project decision or revision log so future agents do not regenerate them.
