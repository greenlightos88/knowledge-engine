# Acceptance Gates

No artifact is approved solely because it is fluent, complete or formatted correctly. Apply the gates relevant to the deliverable.

Use ratings:

- **PASS:** works without material repair;
- **REVISE:** viable but contains a named defect;
- **FAIL:** underlying mechanism is absent or contradictory;
- **N/A:** genuinely irrelevant to this artifact.

## Gate 1 — Intent

- The deliverable and intended audience are clear.
- The work creates a specific audience effect rather than generic engagement.
- The execution matches the approved creative direction.

## Gate 2 — Canon and truth

- No assertion contradicts higher-authority canon.
- Proposals, inferences and facts are distinguishable.
- Research-dependent claims have provenance or are flagged.
- New binding decisions are recorded.

## Gate 3 — Causality and structure

- Major beats connect through consequence and choice.
- The protagonist materially affects the path.
- Escalation changes pressure, not merely intensity.
- Setups function in the present and payoffs create consequence.
- The ending resolves the central dramatic pressure through action.

## Gate 4 — Character

- Important characters possess active objectives.
- Behaviour follows specific appraisal, history and coping strategy.
- Contradictions generate decisions.
- Relationships contain leverage, asymmetry and change.
- Characters are distinguishable beyond biography or surface traits.

## Gate 5 — Scene

- Each substantial scene has objective, resistance, adaptation and change.
- The scene enters at the useful pressure point and exits after consequence.
- Exposition is active and timed to need.
- Removing the scene would materially damage causality, character or audience understanding.

## Gate 6 — Dialogue

- Lines perform actions rather than summarize meaning.
- Subtext is grounded in objectives and risk.
- Voices differ in rhythm, register, attention and avoidance.
- Characters do not share identical emotional fluency.
- Redundant greetings, labels, restatements and exposition are removed.

## Gate 7 — Pacing and information

- The rate of meaningful change suits the intended experience.
- Repetition has transformed meaning or has been removed.
- Audience knowledge is deliberately controlled.
- Reveals alter strategy, interpretation or consequence.
- Relief beats deepen attachment, consequence or anticipation.

## Gate 8 — Cinematic execution

- Internal meaning is translated into playable behaviour and sensory design.
- Geography and action are legible.
- Images and sounds have dramatic function.
- Description is precise without directing every shot.
- Production implications are understandable.

## Gate 9 — Human authorship quality

This gate is mandatory for all finished narrative prose, dialogue and screenplay pages. Run the full rubric in `human-writing/human-writing-gate.md`.

- A particular consciousness governs attention and selection.
- Emotional meaning is carried by strategy, contradiction, timing and consequence rather than stock physical shorthand.
- Characters differ in thought pattern, articulation, social tactic and use of silence.
- Specificity is local, functional and difficult to transfer unchanged to another project.
- Rhythm responds to consciousness and pressure rather than manufactured intensity.
- The audience is allowed to infer without losing essential causality.
- Surprising choices are earned by character, world and consequence.
- No automatic rejection condition from the Human Writing Gate remains unresolved.

A fluent page that fails this gate is not approved.

## Gate 10 — Originality and anti-AI quality

- The work is not assembled from default genre beats without transformation.
- Specificity arises from character, world, causality and worldview.
- Prose avoids generic "cinematic" adjectives, fake profundity and uniform rhythm.
- Mystery is meaningful rather than vague.
- The work leaves appropriate negative space and does not explain every inference.
- References have been translated into abstract attributes rather than copied.

## Gate 11 — Production fitness

- Format and length are appropriate.
- Constraints are acknowledged and creatively integrated.
- Actor intention and scene circumstances are playable.
- Stunt, VFX, sound, location and cast demands are coherent.
- The artifact is clear enough for its next professional reader.
