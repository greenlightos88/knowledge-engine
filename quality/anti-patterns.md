# Anti-Patterns and Repair Strategies

These patterns are symptoms. Diagnose the underlying dramatic failure before rewriting prose.

## Generic cinematic prose

**Symptoms:** "the air hangs heavy," "a beat," "something shifts," excessive fragments, empty references to darkness, silence or eyes.

**Cause:** abstract mood replacing observed action and specific circumstance.

**Repair:** name the concrete behaviour, object, spatial change or sound that carries the mood and affects attention.

## Emotional explanation

**Symptoms:** action lines or dialogue naming exactly what a character feels and why.

**Cause:** insufficient trust in performance or weak behavioural design.

**Repair:** create a choice, failed task, avoidance pattern, contradiction or physical consequence that makes the emotion inferable.

## Exposition relay

**Symptoms:** characters politely exchange information for the audience.

**Cause:** information has no tactical function.

**Repair:** make the information contested, withheld, dangerous, useful, misinterpreted or necessary to an immediate decision.

## Identical voices

**Symptoms:** all characters use the same vocabulary, sentence rhythm, wit and emotional intelligence.

**Cause:** authorial voice has replaced character strategy.

**Repair:** define each speaker's objective, register, avoidance, metaphor source, status response and stress degradation.

## Escalation by volume

**Symptoms:** repeated threats, louder arguments, more attackers, larger spectacle without changed meaning.

**Cause:** the pressure dimension remains static.

**Repair:** change leverage, cost, time, exposure, allegiance, moral compromise, geography or objective.

## Passive protagonist

**Symptoms:** protagonist receives lore, survives attacks and follows instructions while others drive the plot.

**Cause:** premise event mistaken for sustained agency.

**Repair:** force decisions with credible alternatives and make consequences flow from the protagonist's chosen strategy.

## Mystery as vagueness

**Symptoms:** undefined ominous behaviour, withheld basics, random symbols and delayed answers with no usable pattern.

**Cause:** absence of designed information architecture.

**Repair:** define the hidden truth, audience hypothesis path, clues, misinterpretations and consequence of each reveal.

## Trauma as character

**Symptoms:** biography explains all behaviour; suffering substitutes for desire, humour, competence and contradiction.

**Cause:** backstory mistaken for personality.

**Repair:** define present objectives, coping strategy, appetites, social behaviour, values and choices unrelated to the trauma.

## Theme speech

**Symptoms:** characters articulate the story's argument in polished monologues.

**Cause:** thematic conflict has not been embedded in action.

**Repair:** place competing values into choices where every option costs something.

## Arbitrary twist

**Symptoms:** surprise depends on hidden facts that could not be inferred or contradict prior behaviour.

**Cause:** surprise prioritized over re-interpretation.

**Repair:** seed observable evidence with an alternate initial meaning; make the reveal alter past and future causality.

## Artificial prestige

**Symptoms:** obscurity, misery, slow pacing, symbolic imagery or ambiguous endings used as automatic markers of seriousness.

**Cause:** surface traits substituted for depth.

**Repair:** ground restraint and ambiguity in character pressure, thematic conflict and precise consequence.

## Over-designed completeness

**Symptoms:** every symbol explained, every relationship resolved, every emotion named, every scene tied with a verbal bow.

**Cause:** fear that the audience will miss meaning.

**Repair:** preserve legible inference. End on consequence, image or behaviour when explanation adds no new dramatic information.
