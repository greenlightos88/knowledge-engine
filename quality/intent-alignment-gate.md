# Intent Alignment Gate

## Purpose

Determine whether a recommendation, draft, revision, or generated artifact succeeds on the project's own declared terms.

This gate runs before prestige-oriented craft preferences and before final acceptance.

## Required inputs

- approved Creative Success Profile;
- current project canon;
- target audience and format;
- genre contract;
- production constraints;
- current artifact.

If the Creative Success Profile is missing, the system must establish one before making major tonal or structural revisions.

## Gate sequence

### 1. Intent preservation

- Does the artifact remain recognizably the project the creator requested?
- Have any non-negotiable qualities been diluted?
- Has the work been made more respectable at the cost of identity?

### 2. Audience promise

- Is the intended audience experience legible?
- Does the opening establish permission for sincerity, camp, absurdity, vulgarity, melodrama, or tonal excess?
- Are expectations being fulfilled, escalated, or deliberately subverted?

### 3. Weighted success

Evaluate only the dimensions weighted in the Creative Success Profile.

Do not penalize a project for low character depth, realism, thematic complexity, restraint, or subtlety when those dimensions were intentionally assigned low or zero weight.

### 4. Controlled execution

Intentional stupidity is not an exemption from control.

Test:

- clarity of premise;
- pace;
- timing;
- escalation;
- payoff;
- internal rules appropriate to the mode;
- tonal consistency;
- production feasibility;
- performer opportunity;
- memorable moments.

### 5. Sincerity integrity

- Does the project believe in itself to the declared degree?
- Is self-awareness helping or weakening the effect?
- Are characters mocking the premise merely because the writer lacks confidence?
- Is camp being played with sufficient commitment?

### 6. Repair alignment

For every proposed change, state:

- the failure being repaired;
- the project-specific criterion affected;
- why the repair strengthens intent;
- what identity risk the repair introduces.

## Automatic rejection conditions

Reject a recommendation when it:

- adds seriousness only to signal quality;
- replaces broad comedy with tasteful understatement without authorization;
- removes vulgarity, excess, gore, sentimentality, melodrama, or cliché that is functioning as intended;
- inserts trauma or backstory to legitimize a simple entertainment premise;
- slows a fast project for unrequested contemplation;
- mistakes archetype for underdevelopment when archetype is the chosen mode;
- treats a knowingly ridiculous premise as a defect to conceal;
- introduces constant ironic dialogue that destroys investment;
- applies another genre's success criteria.

## Scoring

Use four outcomes:

- **PASS:** strongly serves declared intent.
- **PASS WITH RISKS:** serves intent but creates identified tradeoffs.
- **REVISE:** meaningful misalignment or weak delivery.
- **REJECT:** optimizes against the project or substitutes agent taste for creator intent.

## Final test

Ask:

> Did this change make the work more effectively itself?

If the answer is no, the change does not pass.
