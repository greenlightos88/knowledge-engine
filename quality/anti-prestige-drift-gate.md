# Anti-Prestige Drift Gate

## Status

Required whenever a project values comedy, pulp, camp, exploitation, melodrama, absurdity, deliberate cheapness, simplicity, vulgarity, spectacle, or archetypal storytelling.

## Purpose

Detect revisions that appear sophisticated but weaken the project's declared identity or audience pleasure.

## Automatic failure conditions

Fail the recommendation when it:

- adds trauma only to make a comic or pulp character respectable;
- reduces joke size, vulgarity, speed, colour, violence, or excess without project-specific cause;
- replaces a clear premise with ambiguity for status;
- inserts thematic dialogue that interrupts entertainment;
- treats archetype as a defect merely because it is not psychologically dense;
- forces realism onto a world whose pleasure depends on heightened causality;
- makes characters wink at the premise to excuse it;
- removes a familiar convention without providing an equal or stronger function;
- slows the story to signal seriousness;
- converts deliberate cheapness into generic polish;
- changes a happy, outrageous, cruel, triumphant, or ridiculous ending into bittersweet restraint without mandate;
- critiques the audience's pleasure instead of improving delivery of that pleasure.

## Evaluation sequence

For every substantial recommendation:

1. identify the declared project intention;
2. identify the target audience pleasure;
3. state the defect being repaired;
4. state the doctrine used;
5. test whether the recommendation imports values from another mode;
6. identify what project-specific quality improves;
7. identify what may be lost;
8. reject or revise the recommendation if identity loss exceeds functional gain.

## Prestige substitution test

Ask whether the proposed change is substituting any of the following for actual improvement:

- solemnity for consequence;
- ambiguity for complexity;
- trauma for characterization;
- slowness for confidence;
- silence for subtext;
- desaturation for taste;
- discomfort for meaning;
- bleakness for maturity;
- self-awareness for intelligence;
- realism for coherence;
- topicality for relevance;
- explanation of harm for moral seriousness.

## Protected qualities

Project-specific protected qualities may include:

- stupidity;
- innocence;
- vulgarity;
- cheapness;
- speed;
- repetition;
- theatricality;
- melodrama;
- sentimentality;
- archetype;
- spectacle;
- cruelty;
- nonsense;
- formula;
- escapism;
- wish fulfilment;
- uncomplicated victory.

Protection does not exempt execution from critique. It prevents critique from deleting the intended source of pleasure.

## Repair rule

When a weakness is real, repair it within the project's mode.

Examples:

- weak broad comedy → improve setup, contrast, escalation, timing, target, or consequence;
- weak B-movie → clarify premise delivery, danger, set pieces, pacing, and production ingenuity;
- weak camp → increase commitment, control, specificity, image, rhythm, and quotability;
- weak melodrama → strengthen emotional legibility, reversals, sacrifice, and payoff;
- weak absurdism → strengthen pattern, rule transformation, image logic, and conceptual escalation.

Do not repair one mode by converting it into another.

## Acceptance test

Pass only when the recommendation makes the work more effective on its declared terms and preserves or intensifies the qualities the audience came to receive.
