# Wow Readiness Scorecard

## Purpose

The Knowledge Engine must not claim excellence from architecture alone. It earns readiness through repeatable, creator-visible outcomes.

Score each dimension from 0 to 10 using evidence from real executions. A 10 means the dimension is demonstrably excellent for the declared task, not theoretically possible.

## Dimensions

### 1. Intent fidelity

Does the result solve the creator's deeper objective without betraying explicit instructions, anti-goals, or project identity?

### 2. Canon and continuity integrity

Does the result preserve authoritative truth, distinguish inference from fact, and identify every material downstream impact?

### 3. Creative causality

Are story, character, emotion, suspense, comedy, and spectacle driven by intelligible causes and choices rather than decoration?

### 4. Human writing quality

Does the writing feel authored by a particular consciousness, with specificity, differentiated voices, meaningful omission, living rhythm, and no generic model residue?

### 5. Audience design

Is attention, prediction, comprehension, emotion, surprise, memory, and payoff deliberately controlled according to the project's intended experience?

### 6. Production usefulness

Can actors, directors, producers, and departments understand and use the artifact? Does it preserve value while respecting real constraints?

### 7. Long-horizon coherence

Does the result strengthen the whole project rather than only the local passage? Are setup, payoff, arcs, dependencies, and future consequences accounted for?

### 8. Taste fit

Does the result match the creator's approved preferences while respecting project-specific overrides and avoiding imitation or prestige drift?

### 9. Trust and transparency

Are confidence, assumptions, evidence, open questions, rejected alternatives, validation results, and repair history visible and honest?

### 10. Usability and momentum

Does the system reduce friction, avoid repeated explanation, present the right amount of information, and leave the creator with a clear next action?

## Scoring anchors

- **0–2:** unusable or materially misaligned
- **3–4:** partial value; major repair required
- **5–6:** competent but ordinary
- **7–8:** strong professional result
- **9:** exceptional and reliably approval-ready
- **10:** creator-validated benchmark result that is difficult to improve without changing intent

## Release rule

An artifact may be approval-ready only when:

- no blocking gate fails;
- Intent Fidelity and Canon Integrity score at least 9;
- no other applicable dimension scores below 8;
- all uncertainty that could change the artifact is disclosed;
- repository impact is classified;
- the artifact survives an adversarial critique pass.

## Benchmark protocol

Maintain a benchmark suite containing:

- local scene rewrite;
- full scene creation from canon;
- character dossier;
- story architecture diagnosis;
- dialogue and subtext repair;
- production feasibility transformation;
- studio bible section;
- cross-document canon refactor;
- intentionally camp or lowbrow execution;
- ambiguous creator request requiring intent reconstruction.

For each benchmark, retain:

- input request;
- source context hash;
- Intent Contract;
- model and configuration;
- council passes used;
- artifact;
- validation and repair record;
- creator rating;
- creator corrections;
- final accepted version.

## No false tens

A score of 10 cannot be assigned by the engine alone. It requires creator approval or repeated benchmark evidence showing no material correction was needed.
