# Operating Protocol

## Purpose

This protocol turns an AI model into a repeatable story-development process. It prevents blank-page generation, canon drift, undiagnosed rewrites and endless taste-based iteration.

## Intake contract

Every task should resolve the following before substantial drafting:

- **Deliverable:** scene, outline, treatment, screenplay, bible, pitch, notes or research.
- **Audience:** creator, actor, producer, financier, department head or general viewer.
- **Format:** feature, short, vertical episode, series, prose or hybrid.
- **Stage:** discovery, architecture, drafting, diagnosis, revision or packaging.
- **Authority:** exploratory, proposed, approved or locked.
- **Constraints:** length, budget, location, cast, rating, culture, language and deadline.
- **Target effect:** what the audience should anticipate, feel, understand or question.

When information is missing, infer only what can be safely inferred and label it. Do not stall a task over details that can be resolved through a reversible proposal.

## Development loop

### 1. Orient

Read the source hierarchy. Build a compact state model containing premise, protagonist, central pressure, controlling theme question, current structure, canon constraints and open decisions.

### 2. Diagnose

Name the actual problem before changing pages. Distinguish among premise, causality, agency, stakes, character, pacing, information, tone, dialogue, continuity and formatting defects.

### 3. Design

Generate no more than three materially different solutions. Evaluate each by dramatic power, originality, canon impact, setup cost, production cost and downstream consequences.

### 4. Decide

Select or recommend one solution. Record any canon-changing choice. Do not blend incompatible options into a compromise that loses the purpose of both.

### 5. Execute

Draft at the requested level. Preserve uncertainty where the story benefits from uncertainty; remove uncertainty where the production team needs precision.

### 6. Attack

Run an adversarial review. Ask what is generic, convenient, unearned, repetitive, over-explained, culturally unsupported or impossible to stage.

### 7. Refine

Revise the dramatic mechanism first, then the prose. A sentence-level pass cannot rescue a scene with no choice, pressure or turn.

### 8. Lock

Mark accepted decisions, update continuity and identify the next dependency.

## Context loading rule

Load only the knowledge needed for the current task. More context is not automatically better. Prioritize high-authority, task-relevant files and summarize them into a working brief before reasoning.

## Change control

A change is **local** when it affects wording or execution without changing story truth. A change is **architectural** when it alters motive, causality, timeline, mythology, relationship, theme, reveal order or ending. Architectural changes require a dependency scan and decision-log entry.

## Failure recovery

When output feels "AI-written," do not simply request more grit, poetry or realism. Diagnose the cause:

- abstraction instead of observed detail;
- explanation instead of behavior;
- familiar beat sequencing;
- uniform sentence rhythm;
- no meaningful character resistance;
- no irreversible turn;
- borrowed genre shorthand;
- excessive completeness that removes negative space.

Repair the mechanism that caused the smell.
