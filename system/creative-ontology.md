# Creative Reasoning Ontology

This ontology defines how reality becomes a production-ready dramatic artifact. It prevents agents from treating prose generation as the beginning of creative work.

## Primary chain

```text
Reality and context
→ perception and attention
→ appraisal and meaning
→ emotion and physiological state
→ desire, need and prediction
→ decision under constraint
→ behaviour and communication
→ relationship and power change
→ conflict and consequence
→ story architecture
→ scene mechanism
→ dialogue, image and sound
→ audience cognition
→ production execution
→ evaluation and revision
```

## Layer responsibilities

### 1. Reality and context
Defines material conditions, culture, history, environment, institutions, resources and constraints. No psychological or dramatic claim may contradict established reality without an explicit speculative rule.

### 2. Human mechanics
Explains how a person notices, interprets, remembers, predicts, regulates, chooses and acts. Character behaviour must be traceable through this layer.

### 3. Relationships and social systems
Models attachment, status, belonging, obligation, trust, rivalry, hierarchy, cooperation and group pressure. Conflict is relational before it is structural.

### 4. Dramatic architecture
Selects and compresses behaviour into causally linked changes. It governs pressure, agency, escalation, reversals, consequence, revelation and payoff.

### 5. Expressive translation
Converts dramatic mechanisms into scene action, dialogue, image, sound, rhythm, silence, objects, space and performance opportunity.

### 6. Audience cognition
Tracks attention, prediction, comprehension, uncertainty, empathy, dread, surprise, relief, memory and meaning. Intended audience effect must be designed rather than assumed.

### 7. Production intelligence
Tests feasibility, budget pressure, location, cast, safety, scheduling, performance demands, editorial options and delivery format without reducing artistic ambition to convenience.

### 8. Evaluation and revision
Diagnoses the governing failure, selects doctrine, repairs the mechanism, checks downstream effects and repeats until acceptance.

## Directional rules

- Lower layers may express higher layers but may not invent unsupported causes for them.
- Prose polish cannot repair missing human or dramatic logic.
- Structural changes require a backward scan into motive, relationship and reality, and a forward scan into scenes, audience effect and production.
- Production constraints may reshape expression, but changes to meaning or canon must be explicit.
- Audience effect is evidence of mechanism, not a substitute for mechanism.

## Traceability requirement

For every major creative decision, the system should be able to state:

1. what reality or canon it depends on;
2. what the character perceives and believes;
3. what pressure alters the decision;
4. what behaviour follows;
5. what relationship or possibility changes;
6. what the audience experiences;
7. what production must execute;
8. which gates verify success.

A decision that cannot be traced is a proposal, not established architecture.
