# Intent Reconstruction Standard

## Purpose

The Knowledge Engine must solve the creator's actual objective, not merely obey the surface wording of a request.

Intent reconstruction is a bounded reasoning stage that converts an incomplete or conversational instruction into an explicit execution contract without inventing new canon, changing the creator's taste, or silently expanding scope.

## Required output

Before drafting, the orchestrator produces an Intent Contract with:

- **surface request** — what the creator literally asked for;
- **deeper objective** — the result the creator is trying to achieve;
- **audience effect** — what the audience should feel, understand, anticipate, or remember;
- **artifact target** — the exact deliverable and format;
- **project identity to preserve** — tone, genre contract, sincerity mode, signature mechanisms, and non-negotiables;
- **constraints** — canon, production, continuity, length, budget, platform, cultural, legal, and approval constraints;
- **anti-goals** — outcomes that would technically satisfy the wording but betray the request;
- **success conditions** — observable criteria for acceptance;
- **open uncertainties** — unresolved facts that materially affect execution;
- **assumption policy** — which low-risk assumptions may be made and which require creator approval.

## Reconstruction rules

1. Preserve explicit instructions over inferred preferences.
2. Preserve project canon over generic craft advice.
3. Infer only from durable project evidence, creator taste records, decision logs, and the current request.
4. Do not turn a genre piece into prestige drama unless that is the declared intent.
5. Do not mistake brevity for low ambition. A short request may imply a high-standard deliverable.
6. Do not ask a clarifying question when the repository contains the answer or when a reversible, low-risk assumption can be declared.
7. Do not conceal assumptions. Place them in the execution record.
8. When multiple interpretations remain viable, select the one best supported by project identity and state the alternative not chosen.

## Failure conditions

Intent reconstruction fails when:

- the proposed objective contradicts the creator's explicit request;
- the engine optimizes for generic model taste;
- success criteria are vague or purely aesthetic;
- a material assumption is treated as fact;
- the artifact target is undefined;
- the engine cannot distinguish canon preservation from creative invention.

## Execution contract example

```yaml
surface_request: Rewrite the shrine scene.
deeper_objective: Make the scene psychologically terrifying, culturally grounded, actor-playable, and producer-readable while preserving the covenant cosmology.
audience_effect:
  - curiosity becomes dread
  - the supernatural feels causal rather than decorative
  - the scene creates a memorable irreversible choice
artifact_target: revised Fountain scene plus synchronization report
preserve:
  - Efik cultural specificity
  - Kai's curiosity-driven agency
  - covenant cosmology
  - restrained exposition
constraints:
  - existing location
  - producible effects
  - no canon contradiction
anti_goals:
  - generic haunted-temple imagery
  - lore monologue
  - passive protagonist
success_conditions:
  - Kai makes a legible but emotionally credible choice
  - every supernatural beat changes behaviour
  - dialogue can be removed without losing scene comprehension
  - downstream continuity impacts are identified
```

## Relationship to approval

Intent reconstruction is advisory and traceable. It never changes Canon. The creator approves artifacts and material decisions through the existing approval path.
