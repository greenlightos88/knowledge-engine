# Tone Calibration Standard

## Status

Canonical, repository-wide.

## Purpose

Translate vague tone labels into executable choices across writing, performance, image, sound, pacing, consequence, and audience permission.

## Governing principle

Tone is not an adjective. Tone is the stable relationship between subject matter, presentation, consequence, performance, and audience expectation.

## Tone dimensions

Every project should calibrate at least the following dimensions:

```yaml
tone:
  sincerity: 0-10
  realism: 0-10
  emotional_intensity: 0-10
  comic_density: 0-10
  vulgarity: 0-10
  violence: 0-10
  grotesquerie: 0-10
  sentimentality: 0-10
  self_awareness: 0-10
  stylization: 0-10
  narrative_speed: 0-10
  consequence_severity: 0-10
  ambiguity: 0-10
  warmth: 0-10
```

Numbers are calibration aids, not objective truth. Each important dimension must include a sentence explaining what the selected level means in practice.

## Register map

Tone must be translated into choices for:

- narrator or screenplay voice;
- dialogue rhythm;
- character awareness;
- performance scale;
- reaction behaviour;
- visual composition;
- production design;
- sound and music;
- editing rhythm;
- injury and consequence;
- sexuality and vulgarity;
- emotional release;
- ending treatment.

## Sincerity rules

Sincerity and absurdity are independent.

A ridiculous premise may be played with complete emotional commitment. A realistic premise may be treated as parody. The engine must not assume that bizarre material requires winking dialogue or that comedy requires characters to know they are funny.

## Tone boundaries

Every project must identify:

- allowed extremes;
- forbidden tonal moves;
- moments permitted to break the dominant register;
- who or what carries sincerity;
- what cannot become a joke;
- what cannot become solemn;
- how consequences behave;
- how the project returns to baseline after deviation.

## Tonal collision protocol

When a scene feels wrong, diagnose the specific collision:

- performance scale versus consequence;
- joke density versus emotional processing;
- music versus image;
- violence versus reaction behaviour;
- vulgarity versus audience age;
- self-awareness versus investment;
- realism versus premise tolerance;
- sentiment versus established restraint;
- spectacle versus production world.

Do not repair tone by making every element moderate. Resolve which signal governs and recalibrate the others around it.

## Tone drift

Tone drift occurs when repeated local choices alter the project's operating agreement without an intentional transition.

Common causes:

- agents adding prestige seriousness;
- quips eroding danger;
- realism suddenly policing a previously heightened world;
- consequence appearing only when convenient;
- sentimental scenes importing a different movie;
- characters becoming self-aware to excuse an implausible premise;
- escalating vulgarity without changing comic function;
- visual polish contradicting deliberate cheapness.

## Acceptance test

Tone calibration passes when a writer, actor, director, editor, and producer could make compatible choices from the same document without reducing the work to a single adjective.
