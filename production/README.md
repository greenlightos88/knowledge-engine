# Production Intelligence

## Purpose
Translate creative reasoning into choices that can be performed, photographed, recorded, edited, financed, scheduled, packaged, and communicated.

Production intelligence does not reduce art to logistics. It identifies how practical constraints alter the audience experience and how creative intent can be protected through execution.

## Governing principle
A choice is not production-ready because it is imaginable. It is production-ready when its dramatic function, physical requirements, risks, dependencies, alternatives, and expected audience value are understood.

## Core domains

- format and duration
- budget band
- cast and performer requirements
- location and company moves
- blocking and spatial clarity
- stunt and intimacy demands
- practical effects and VFX
- props, wardrobe, hair and makeup
- sound and music strategy
- cinematography requirements
- editorial dependencies
- legal, cultural and safety constraints
- schedule pressure
- platform and delivery requirements
- market and package legibility

## Creative value model
For every costly or difficult element, identify:

```yaml

dramatic_function: ""
audience_value: ""

minimum_viable_execution: ""
preferred_execution: ""
low_cost_alternative: ""

```

## Production-value questions

1. What will the audience actually perceive?
2. Does the element create story value, emotional value, spectacle, comedy, atmosphere, clarity, star opportunity, or marketing value?
3. Can the same function be achieved more effectively through performance, sound, framing, editing, implication, or location choice?
4. Is the expensive element isolated enough to execute well?
5. Does the scene require resources the project has not declared?
6. Does simplifying the production strengthen or weaken the intended experience?

## Constraint transformation
A constraint should be processed as:

```text
Constraint
→ threatened creative function
→ minimum experience that must survive
→ available production variables
→ redesigned execution
→ audience-effect verification
```

## Required outputs
The production layer should be able to project project knowledge into:

- screenplay production pass
- scene feasibility report
- actor packet
- director packet
- location brief
- stunt brief
- prop and continuity brief
- VFX and practical-effects notes
- sound and music intent
- schedule-risk report
- pitch and packaging materials

## Format awareness
A feature, vertical short, proof of concept, episodic series, microbudget film, stage piece, and audio drama require different scene density, visual strategy, production assumptions, and audience retention models. The format must be declared before production evaluation.

## Performance protection
Do not simplify away the performer opportunity that makes a scene valuable. Identify the playable objective, turn, physical action, emotional exposure, comic mechanism, or star moment that must remain.

## Anti-false-economy rule
A cheaper version is not automatically better. Removing the central pleasure, visual promise, joke, scare, fight idea, creature reveal, or emotional event can make the entire project economically pointless.

## Acceptance test
A production recommendation passes when it protects the declared audience experience, exposes real dependencies, offers executable alternatives, and makes the value-to-cost tradeoff explicit.
