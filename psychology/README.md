# Psychology Engine

The Psychology Engine converts inner human processes into observable dramatic behaviour.

It is not a diagnostic manual, a personality quiz, or a substitute for clinical expertise. Its function is creative: to help writers and agents build behaviour that feels caused, specific, contradictory, and playable.

## Core model

A dramatic behaviour chain should be traceable:

```text
stimulus
→ appraisal
→ felt stakes
→ impulse
→ inhibition or defence
→ chosen behaviour
→ consequence
→ updated belief
```

Writers may compress or conceal this chain on the page, but the underlying logic must exist.

## Governing laws

1. **Appraisal before emotion.** Events do not create identical feelings in everyone. Meaning creates emotion.
2. **Behaviour before explanation.** Internal state should become choice, attention, speech, posture, avoidance, ritual, error, or sacrifice.
3. **Protection before honesty.** Under pressure, people usually protect identity, attachment, status, safety, or control before they state the truth.
4. **Contradiction is functional.** Opposing impulses reveal competing needs, not random inconsistency.
5. **Coping creates plot.** The strategy that once protected a character should eventually generate cost.
6. **Relationships regulate behaviour.** People change depending on who is present, what can be lost, and who holds leverage.
7. **Emotion changes perception.** Fear, shame, desire, grief, and status threat alter what a character notices and misreads.

## Use in story development

For every principal character, define:

- what they believe is happening;
- what they believe it means about them;
- what they stand to lose;
- what they want immediately;
- what they cannot admit;
- how they usually protect themselves;
- how that protection harms them or others;
- what evidence could force their model of reality to change.

Psychology is approved only when it produces causally legible behaviour and dramatic consequence.
