# Revision Protocol

## Principle

Revision is controlled problem-solving. Do not rewrite everything because the pages feel wrong. Name the defect, locate its cause, choose the smallest change capable of solving it, then test downstream effects.

## Step 1 — Preserve strengths

Before notes, record what must survive: images, performances, relationships, turns, tone, cultural specificity, production advantages and audience effects.

## Step 2 — Diagnose by layer

Classify every note:

- premise;
- structure and causality;
- character and relationship;
- scene function;
- pacing and information;
- tone and genre;
- dialogue and voice;
- continuity and canon;
- production or format;
- prose and formatting.

A note such as "the middle drags" is an observation, not a diagnosis. Identify repeated tactics, static objectives, delayed consequences, redundant information or absent choice.

## Step 3 — Find the earliest cause

The visible failure often occurs after its cause. A weak climax may originate in an undefined threshold in Act One. A flat reveal may originate in absent audience hypotheses. Repair the earliest causal defect.

## Step 4 — Rank changes

Use priority:

1. blocking contradiction;
2. architecture and ending;
3. protagonist agency and character motive;
4. sequence and scene function;
5. information and pacing;
6. dialogue and cinematic execution;
7. polish and format.

## Step 5 — Propose solutions

Offer up to three materially different solutions with:

- mechanism;
- benefit;
- cost;
- canon impact;
- setup required;
- downstream files affected.

Recommend one. Do not blend options automatically.

## Step 6 — Build a change map

```text
DEFECT:
ROOT CAUSE:
APPROVED SOLUTION:
EARLIEST AFFECTED BEAT:
SCENES TO ADD:
SCENES TO REMOVE:
SCENES TO REWRITE:
CANON FILES TO UPDATE:
CONTINUITY RISKS:
STRENGTHS TO PRESERVE:
ACCEPTANCE TEST:
```

## Step 7 — Execute in dependency order

Revise outlines and canon before dependent pages. Keep unrelated material unchanged. Do not introduce unapproved mythology, relationships or backstory while solving a local defect.

## Step 8 — Regression test

After revision verify:

- the named defect is actually solved;
- no setup or payoff is orphaned;
- character knowledge remains coherent;
- timeline and geography remain possible;
- later choices still follow;
- tone and production scope remain controlled;
- strengths identified in Step 1 survived.

## Step 9 — Record

Log architectural decisions, deprecated alternatives and unresolved questions. Mark the artifact status and next dependency.
